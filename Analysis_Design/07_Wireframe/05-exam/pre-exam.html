Instructions to candidates

IELTS Academic Listening

Time: 32 minutes · 40 questions · 4 parts

  • Answer all the questions.
  • You can change your answers at any time during the test.
  • You will hear each part once only. The recording cannot be paused or replayed.
  • At the end you have 2 minutes to check your answers.
  • Each question carries one mark.

IELTS Academic Reading

Time: 60 minutes · 40 questions · 3 parts

  • Answer all the questions.
  • You can change your answers at any time during the test.
  • Select text in a passage and right-click to highlight it or add a note.
  • Each question carries one mark.

The clock starts when you click Start test and keeps running even if you close the page. When the time is up, your answers are submitted.
